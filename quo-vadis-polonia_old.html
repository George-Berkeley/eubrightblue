Overview

Executive summary

A concise overview of why Brexit matters for Poland’s strategic choices and how Warsaw can build greater influence and resilience inside Europe.

Core Takeaways

  • Sovereignty vs. influence: Leaving EU decision-making structures carries practical costs for shaping rules that still affect a country’s economy and security.
  • Economic & strategic stakes: Deep European integration underpins trade, investment, and regional security in ways difficult to replicate outside.
  • A constructive path forward: Focuses on practical reforms and stronger European partnerships to keep Poland secure and influential.
